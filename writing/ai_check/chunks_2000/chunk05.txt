4.5 Housing and the homelessness loop

Housing is the four-point grid of (10): h1 is literal homelessness, sleeping rough or in an emergency shelter, which is free, and h2 < h3 < h4 are three rental sizes. Homelessness raises mortality (20), worsens both employment margins (21) and lowers the chance of recovering from the SUD (18), but does not affect SMI (Section 4.7).

The child’s productivity penalty. A child whose parent had the disorder at any point while the child lived at home, before JK or before the parent died if that came first, starts adult life with its productivity lowered by the proportion δd in (22) below, and the penalty stays with the child for life.

A child’s log productivity at entry is drawn around its parent’s, with persistence ρ. The innovation keeps the spread of productivity at entry the same in every generation. If the parent had the disorder at any point before the child became an adult, the penalty scales the child’s productivity down for life:
