Under Treatment First the programme is conditional. A household fails the condition if it uses, v > 0, or has the disorder and is not in treatment, d = 2 and τ = 0. A household with a SUD must be in treatment (paid for by the government), as the executive order directs, and no household may use even while in treatment, as the guidance implementing the order requires of drug-free housing.22

Income-eligible holders and income-eligible households that draw a voucher are tested, at a cost ctest each to the government, and one that fails the condition is detected with probability pdet. This means that enforcement is not perfect: with probability 1−pdet > 0 a household that fails the condition actually gets to keep its voucher. A detected holder keeps this period’s payment and loses the voucher from the next period; a detected applicant never receives it. In both cases, the household is excluded from the voucher programme for a period of time (η = 2): it cannot draw a voucher whatever its income, and it returns to the lottery (η = 0) with probability ψx each year.

None of pdet, ψx and ctest binds in the benchmark, where no voucher is conditional, so all three are set with the experiment in Section 6 rather than in the calibration.

5 Calibrating the quantitative model

The model has fifty-nine parameters, some of which vary by age. Thirty-eight are calibrated externally, from data or the literature, and twenty-one internally. The twenty-six that carry this paper’s story are in Table 3, with the moment, source, data and model value behind each, and are discussed below in numbered blocks of parameters that work through similar mechanisms. The other thirty-three, common to most life- cycle models, are in Appendix A to save the reader time; its blocks continue the numbering. Three further parameters govern drug testing and exclusion from the voucher; they play no role in the benchmark and are set with the experiment in Section 6. data/DATA APPENDIX.md in the replication package documents the dataset, year, universe and computation behind every estimate, and the package carries all datasets, so nothing has to be downloaded.
