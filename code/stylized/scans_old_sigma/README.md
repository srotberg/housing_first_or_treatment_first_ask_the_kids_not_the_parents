# Theta scans run before sigma_y was calibrated

These six points were computed with sigma_y set to 0.75 and with lambda targeting
the 33.2 percent conversion from use to disorder. Both were replaced: sigma_y is
now calibrated to the bottom half's share of earnings and comes out at 1.147, and
lambda targets the 3.68 percent adult SUD rate that the quantitative model also
matches. Every number in these files is therefore from a different model and none
of them is comparable with the current scans.

They are kept because they record the shape of the theta frontier under the old
income distribution: the screen on the mortality channel passed up to theta = 0.24
and failed at 0.25, and the drug spending share peaked well under its target
throughout. Under the new income distribution the whole frontier moved down.
