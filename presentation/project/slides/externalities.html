The mechanism

Three externalities a parent counts only through imperfect altruism

Budget

Every dollar spent on drugs, and every dollar lost while a SUD keeps the parent unemployed, is consumption and housing the child goes without.

Housing First leaves it in place

Earnings

A child whose parent had a SUD while the child lived at home starts adult life with lower productivity.

Housing First leaves it in place

Childhood homelessness

Denying assistance to users exposes more of them to the shelter, and a child lives wherever its parent lives.

Many believe Treatment First creates it

Result: Treatment First reduces all three, including childhood homelessness.
