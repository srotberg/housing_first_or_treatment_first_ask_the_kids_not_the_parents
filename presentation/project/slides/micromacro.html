Micro and macro

The trials are right about the households they follow, and the macro effects overturn the verdict

What a trial sees

Those with a SUD when the reform lands lose: 74% are worse off with the price and πV held, as in a trial too small to move either, and 67% once both clear.

What it cannot see

The households the condition deters from ever starting, the vouchers its savings buy for others, and the children's adult lives.

Once those are counted, homelessness and childhood homelessness fall and welfare rises.
