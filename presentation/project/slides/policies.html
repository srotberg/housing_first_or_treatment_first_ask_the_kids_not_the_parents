Motivation

Two ways to give housing assistance

Housing First

Eligible low-income households that draw a voucher keep it with no requirement to stop using drugs.

The benchmark: most US permanent housing funding works this way.

Treatment First

Assistance is conditional on not using drugs and, for those with a substance use disorder (SUD), on being in treatment.

Enforced by an imperfect drug test: some users go undetected.

Housing First or Treatment First? Ask the Kids, Not the Parents

3

Define the two policies precisely, because the model implements exactly these. Housing First is unconditional; Treatment First conditions the voucher on sobriety and, for those with a disorder, on treatment. The screen is imperfect.
