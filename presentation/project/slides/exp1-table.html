Experiment 1 · no externalities

Drug use falls by up to 28%, yet a newborn loses in every case

How to read it. Each row is one way of introducing Treatment First with the children left out: the drug price and the voucher probability πV are held at their Housing First levels or allowed to clear. Bars to the left of zero are falls in drug use (blue) and losses for a newborn (orange).

What it shows. The screen deters a lot of use, but it takes an option from households that weigh the risks correctly, and the budget gives little back: when πV clears, 58% of households gain, by 0.05% on average, and 96% of those with a SUD lose. Moral hazard alone does not justify the screen.
