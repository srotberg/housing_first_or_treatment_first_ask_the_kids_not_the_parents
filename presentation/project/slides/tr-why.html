The transition

Why homelessness does not rise even in the first years

Few lose a voucher

A household with a SUD keeps it by entering treatment and not using, and the screen catches a user 20% of the time: at most 0.13% of households are excluded in any year.

Use falls at once

Households spend less on drugs and have more left for housing, and fewer acquire a SUD and the unemployment that comes with it.

Vouchers are recycled
