Why

A penalty rises with income only if it cannot be insured

Losing a job

A richer household saves more to protect itself, so its loss is roughly proportional to its income. In utility terms it costs a rich household about what it costs a poor one.

Dying

Death wipes out the whole continuation value of being alive, and that loss grows with income. It deters high-income households and not low-income ones.

Life insurance protects descendants, not the household's own life. Take away saving and job loss grows with income too, which is why it reverses the gradient in the right panel.
