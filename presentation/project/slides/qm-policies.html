The two policies

One rationed voucher programme; Treatment First adds a screen

Housing First

Eligible while income is below a share of the median. An eligible household without a voucher draws one with probability πV, and a holder keeps it while eligible.

Treatment First

Holders and new draws are tested. A household fails if it uses, or has a SUD and is not in treatment, which the government pays for. No one may use, even in treatment.

A failure is caught with probability pdet < 1; a caught household loses the voucher and is excluded, returning to the lottery with probability ψx a year.

The rules follow Executive Order 14321 and the guidance that implements it.
