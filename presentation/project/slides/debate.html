The debate: two claims about the recipients

Critics of Housing First

Unconditional assistance creates moral hazard: weaker incentives to stay sober and employed.

More drug use and SUD → more homelessness → a cost borne by those who fund the programme.

Critics of Treatment First

Conditionality delays stable housing for those who need it most; recovering while homeless is harder.

The circle runs the other way, so Housing First even improves the budget.

Both are about the households who receive the assistance. Neither is about their children.

Eide (2020); National Alliance to End Homelessness; Tsemberis et al. (2004); Larimer et al. (2009)
