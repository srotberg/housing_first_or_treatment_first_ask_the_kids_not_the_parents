Both sides of the debate leave out what matters most: what happens to the children of the households a sobriety requirement deters, in what they consume at home, whether they become homeless, and what they earn as adults.

The model agrees with the trials on the households they follow, who lose from the condition. The macro effects the trials cannot capture overturn the conclusion.

Homelessness does not fall along the way unless treatment and sobriety are a real option for those who already have a SUD. A temporary exemption in treatment keeps childhood homelessness from ever rising.
