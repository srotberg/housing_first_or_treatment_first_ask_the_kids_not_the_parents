Main experiment · homelessness

Homelessness falls by 11%, for three reasons

1

Households that stop using spend less on drugs, so more of them can house themselves.

2

Less SUD means less unemployment, and so less homelessness.

3

The vouchers revoked from households with a SUD go, through a higher πV, to other low-income households.

Among households aged 26 to 50, the years a child lives at home, it also falls by 11%: children in a shelter go from 1.03 to 0.92%.
