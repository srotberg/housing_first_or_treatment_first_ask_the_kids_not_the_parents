Where the gain comes from

Three quarters of the newborn’s gain is an externality

How to read it. Each bar is a newborn’s gain, split into four parts that sum to it; below it, the externality’s share of the gain and the parent’s own gain.

What it shows. The childhood part is the largest, because it accrues every year at home while the earnings penalty reaches the child once. With prices held the parent loses; once the budget clears, both the child and the parent gain.
