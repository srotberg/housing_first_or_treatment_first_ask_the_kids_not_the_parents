What randomised trials show, and what they cannot

They find

  • large, persistent gains in housing stability
  • mixed-to-null effects on drug use
  • lower mortality of homeless veterans with mental illness (Costantini, 2025)

They cannot see

  • beyond 6 to 36 months
  • the children’s adult lives
  • households outside the trial
  • the government’s budget

Only one small trial compares a sobriety condition with unconditional housing (Milby et al., 2005). A model can do all four.

Trials are the evidence base for Housing First. Their limitation is scope, not quality: a trial randomises households within a fixed environment, so it ignores exactly the effects that decide the welfare comparison here.
