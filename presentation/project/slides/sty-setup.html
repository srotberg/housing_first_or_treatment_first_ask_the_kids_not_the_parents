A stylized model

Two facts to explain, without preferences that differ by income

Two periods

Adulthood and old age, thirty years each. Productivity y is lognormal with mean one and fixed for life.

One decision

In the first period a household chooses whether to use, how much, and how much to save, at zero interest and without borrowing.

One transfer

Food assistance F(ye) = max{0, c̄ − ϱye}, so consumption stays positive when saving is switched off.
