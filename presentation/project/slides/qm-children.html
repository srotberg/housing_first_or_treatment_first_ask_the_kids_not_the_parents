Children

The child shares the bundle but not the drug, and the parent weighs it by ωc

u = U(XP) + ωc(ξ(j) − 1)U(Xc) + ℵ − ττu

Consumption and housing are shared through the equivalence scale ξ(j): two members while the child is at home, one after. The drug enters the parent's bundle alone.

The penalty δd

If the parent had a SUD at any point while the child lived at home, the child's productivity is lower for life.

Imperfect altruism

With ωc < 1 the parent counts only part of what its drug use and housing cost the child.
