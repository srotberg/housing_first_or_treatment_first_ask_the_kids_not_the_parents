Housing First or Treatment First?*

Dear Shahar,

Thank you for submitting your paper to the Review of Economic Dynamics. I assigned your paper to an AE of the journal who solicited the advice of three reviewers. The referees are somewhat split and have many concerns. R1 recommends a rejection and lists many important shortcomings of the paper. R2 is on the fence, and R3 recommends a revision. The AE is on the fence or slightly negative, not because of the topic but because of the many loose ends in the analysis. After reading their reports and the AE's letter, I do not yet see a clear path for a revision that would lead to a publishable paper. As a result, I have to reject the paper in its current form. However, we would like to leave the door slightly open in case, after reading the comments, you think that writing an almost new paper is something that interests you. If you undertake such a revision, I promise to send the paper back to the same AE, who should be able to use a subset of or all of the previous referees.
 

Best,

Loukas Karabarbounis
