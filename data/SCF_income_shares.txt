WHICH UNIVERSE THE TABLE SHOULD USE IS AN OPEN QUESTION, put to the author on
8 September. The Fortran currently carries the 26-65 column, described as "the
universe the paper already uses for wealth". The MODEL's histogram has no age
restriction at all: it covers every household from model age 1, which is data
age 26, to death. So the two sides are not on the same universe today.

It matters less than it looks for income -- the bottom tenth moves 0.00923 to
0.00938, under two per cent, against a model gap of about forty -- and more for
wealth, where the bottom tenth halves in magnitude. But the model reports
exactly zero at every wealth percentile because no household in it holds
negative net worth, so wealth is a SIGN disagreement and no choice of universe
repairs it.

METHOD. Sort on the variable, cumulate the weight, and split the bucket that
straddles each cut in proportion to the mass taken from it -- the same rule the
Fortran uses when it walks its own histogram, so the two are computed the same
way and not merely described the same way.
